# v16.69-shared — single-listing insert/refresh files the listing under its neighbourhood or town

Found in the North Carolina demo QA (23 Oct): `insertOne` and `refreshOne` (used by the "Listing Verified" webhook, claim approval, publish, owner edits and /admin/insertone) never loaded the neighbourhood/town list. A listing added or refreshed that way was saved with no `hood` until the next full sync (state sites: no town page; city sites: no neighbourhood page). Both now call `loadHoods(DB)` first (cached, so no extra cost).

Shared fix: it also applies to Orlando, Miami and Tampa on their next deploy.
