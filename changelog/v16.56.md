# v16.56-shared — cold-call verifications go through even when the GHL workflow doesn't fire

Eric, 20 Oct: the email-campaign leads keep using codes. For the ~10k cold-call leads, sales fill in **Owner Email** and set **Listing Verification = Listing Verified**. The site should then mark the listing claimed and invite the owner.

## Found during the check

- **Who receives it:** the "Auto Listing Verification" GHL workflow is published in Orlando and Miami, and is meant to call `/webhooks/ghlstatus` → `applyListingVerification`.
- **Live test (Orlando, fake contact "QA Cold Call Test"):** Owner Email set, then Listing Verified, then Pending → Verified twice. The workflow never called the Orlando or the Miami site (live logs).
- **Real misses:** Harrington Hardwood Floors and P Fagan Handyman Services (Orlando, marked Verified on 9 Oct) were never claimed on the site.
- **Miami:** 99 of the first 100 verified contacts are claimed, so the workflow mostly works there. 1 miss: SeaDog International Systems, which has no owner email.
- **Cause unknown:** GHL workflow steps can't be read through the API, so it's not clear why Orlando's workflow doesn't fire.

## Fixed

- **The GHL sync does the job itself** (`lvFix`, up to 10 per page of 100 contacts).
  - It acts on any business contact, or any waiting new-listing contact, that has Listing Verified/Plus/Pro but is missing the `claimed` tag (or `featured`/`premium` for Plus/Pro).
  - Those go through `applyListingVerification`, the same code the webhook runs: claimed tags, owner email, listing refreshed, claim invite.
  - So a verification lands within one sync pass whether or not the workflow fires. The webhook still works when it does fire.
- **The claim invite goes to the Owner Email** sales entered when there is one, otherwise to the business email. Before, it always went to the business email.

- After deploying: the Orlando test contact was caught by the sync, and the invite went to the Owner Email. The two real Orlando misses are caught too. Results are in the status below.
