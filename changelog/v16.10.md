# v16.10-shared (29 Sep 2026)

Fix: approved listings disappeared from the site about 10 minutes after approval.

## What went wrong
- **What happened:** CFL Counseling (Orlando) was approved through GHL, showed on the site, and was gone again after two sync passes.
- **Why:** its CRM contact had been created by the site's login flow, so it carried the `consumer` and `owner-account` tags. The sync's `isPerson` check skips any contact with those tags, even though the approval webhook had also tagged it `business` and `claimed`.
- **The result:** the sync never "touched" the listing, so it was deleted as stale.

## Fix
- A contact tagged `claimed` is now always treated as a business in the sync.
- CFL Counseling was re-added with `/admin/insertone`.
