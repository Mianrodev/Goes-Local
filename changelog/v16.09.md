# v16.09-shared (29 Sep 2026)

New business listing page, built from the mockup Eric approved. The number v16.08 was only used on a test copy.

## Top of the page
- **Photo banner:** the business's cover photo if it has one; otherwise the photo of its neighbourhood. This replaces the thin coloured strip.
- **Name block:** the logo (or the "Unclaimed" picture) overlaps the banner, and badges sit above the name (Verified, Open now, Top Rated, Not yet claimed, labels).
- **Line under the name:** "{type} · {neighbourhood}, {city}".

## Rating box
- **Rating:** a big Google rating and review count. It falls back to the live Google numbers (`gpRating`) when a claimed listing has no rating of its own.
- **"#N in {City}'s Best":** shown only when the business really is in the top 10 for its type. The position is counted with one indexed query that uses the same score as the Best-of lists.
- **Otherwise:** "Est. {year}" for claimed listings.
- **One row of buttons:** Call, Directions, Website and Share. Directions and Share were removed from the Contact box.

## Unclaimed listings
- **Claim banner** ("Is this your business? Claim it free →") moved from the bottom of the sidebar to under the rating. It links straight to that listing's claim form.
- **About text:** written from the listing's own details: type, area, address, rating and phone. It replaces "This page was built from public information…".

## New sections
- **Location:** a Google map (embed, no API key), the address, a "Get directions" button and "Explore more of {neighbourhood} →".
  - The site's security settings now allow `frame-src https://www.google.com`, which the map needs.
- **Hours:** when none are set, a note says "call {phone} to check they're open" instead of showing nothing.

## Removed or hidden
- **"No reviews yet" and "No photos yet"** boxes are hidden until there's something to show, or until the visitor is logged in and can add a review or photo.
- **"Explore more of {neighbourhood}" block** at the bottom removed. The banner and the Location box now cover it.

## Similar businesses
- **Compact cards:** a small picture, the name, rating and area.
- **How matches are chosen:**
  1. Same business type.
  2. If fewer than 2 are found: same category in the same neighbourhood.
  3. If still fewer than 2: anything in the neighbourhood.
- **The heading follows the match:** "More massage spas nearby", "More Beauty & Personal Care in Brickell" or "More in South Beach".
- Each step uses an index. This fixes wrong matches such as a tire shop among med spas.

## Phones
- **Order:** rating and buttons, claim banner, About, Location, Contact, Hours, enquiry form.
- **Open-now badge:** it now appears twice (phone and desktop copies), and both are updated.
