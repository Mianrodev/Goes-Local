# v16.61-shared — one spelling per sub-category

Eric (20 Oct): "Merge the duplicate sub-categories too."

## What changed
- Sub-categories whose names differ only in capital letters or spaces are now one sub-category
  (e.g. Miami "General Contractor" 183 + "General contractor" 180 → "General Contractor" 363).
- The kept spelling is the one with the most capitals (so "HVAC contractor" beats "Hvac Contractor"),
  then the one with more listings. Names with no look-alike are untouched.
- `migrate()` rewrites existing rows once (idempotent): `businesses.sub`, plus `ad_slots`
  (`UPDATE OR IGNORE`, unique cat/sub), `ad_slot_queue` and `category_banners`.
- New rows from the GHL sync, webhooks and imports go through `SUBCANONOF` in `norm()`, so the
  other spelling can't come back. The map is built from the city's own D1 (`loadSubCanon`, cached 10 min).
- Sub-category URLs were already lower-case slugs, so no links change.

## Size of the merge (live, 20 Oct, read-only check)
- Miami: ~190 look-alike pairs. Orlando: 1 ("Legal Services"/"Legal services"). Tampa: none.
- No ad slots, slot queue or banners on Miami use a sub-category, so no paid placement moves.
