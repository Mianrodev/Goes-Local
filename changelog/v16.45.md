# v16.45-shared — live listings stay live while a new "Add your business" form waits

Approved by Eric on 13 Oct ("Yes, keep live listings visible").

## The problem

When the owner of an existing listing filled in "Add your business", the form matched their contact by phone and tagged it "pending-listing". The sync treated any unclaimed contact with that tag as "not a listing". So the live listing disappeared until someone approved the submission in Admin → Pending listings.

Seven listings were hidden this way:
- **Orlando:** Irri Green, ACE Clean Exteriors.
- **Miami:** Trini Salon, Appliancescare, Epee Miami Fencing, Trenchless Drilling, Miami Spa Massage.

## The fix

`isPerson()` in the sync now hides a "pending-listing" contact only when the add-a-business form itself created it, i.e. a brand-new submission whose contact source is "Goes Local add-a-business".

- An existing listing stays live while the owner's new submission waits.
- Approving that submission still updates the details and removes "pending-listing", as before.
- Rules that didn't change:
  - "claimed" always counts as a listing.
  - "consumer"/"owner-account" contacts are still treated as people.

Deleted tags that 0 contacts used:
- **Orlando:** listing-pending, new-listing, new-listing-submission, listing confirmed, miami-directory.
- **Miami:** listing-pending, new-listing, new-listing-submission, listing confirmed.
