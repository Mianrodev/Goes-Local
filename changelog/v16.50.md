# v16.50-shared — two bugs found by the live QA on Orlando

Eric asked on 14 Oct for a full QA on the live site. I added a fake business ("QA Test Business – please ignore") on Orlando with dev1@mianrosystems.com, read the codes from the GHL conversation, and clicked through as the owner on a phone-size screen.

## Worked on the live site (v16.49)

- Add your business → email code → wrong code refused → right code → listing live, owner signed in.
- The "You're live" email arrived.
- The account page shows the checklist, "Edit hours, services & photos" and "Add another location".
- Saving the edit page with the business email the same as the sign-in email works ("Saved. Your listing has been updated."). This is the Sun Kissed fix.
- The Save bar stays at the bottom.
- A second location added from "Add another location" shows under the same account. This is the Mr Jeff case.

## Fixed

1. **"Add another location" didn't fill in the email.**
   - The check in the page script was written as `[^@\s]` inside a template string, so the browser got `[^@s]`. Any email with the letter "s" in it was rejected.
   - The local test email had no "s", so the test passed.
   - The check is now `[^@ ]`.
2. **A new listing from someone already in GHL lost most of what they typed.**
   - When the email already belongs to a GHL contact (any owner adding another location, or anyone who has an account), no contact can be made when the form is sent. `publishListing` then creates the business contact when the listing goes live.
   - That contact got only the name, phone, address and category. The description, services, website, area, year established and owner phone were dropped.
   - Fix:
     - Those details are now kept with the request (new `claims.extra` column, added by migrate).
     - `publishListing` copies them, plus the description, onto the new contact. It uses `ghlUpdateContact`, which saves native and custom fields in two separate calls.
     - It then reads the contact back so the listing goes up complete.
