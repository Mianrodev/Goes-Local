# v16.49-shared — fixes from Sales' first owner calls (Mr Jeff Cleaners, Sun Kissed Tanning)

## Sun Kissed Tanning: "Business email can't be the same as the sign-in email"

The owner signed in with tanning@sktsalon.com, which is also the listing's business email. The edit form shows that email, and the site refused the whole save, so hours and photos couldn't be changed either. This hits every owner who signs in with their business email, which is common, and more so now that codes go to the listing email.
- Now: if the business email box equals the sign-in email, the listing's public email is left as it is (it isn't written to the business contact, to avoid a GHL merge) and everything else saves.
- If the listing showed a different public email, a green note explains it and offers to switch it for them.

## Mr Jeff Cleaners: "claimed but not showing"

Coral Way is owned by connect@laundryus.app (approved 13 Oct), but Sergio signs in as sergio.aguirre@laundryus.app, so he saw nothing.
- **Team access:** `ownedIds()` now also returns listings whose owner email is on the same company domain as the person signed in. Public providers (gmail.com, outlook.com and the rest of FREEMAIL) never count.
- **No colleague disputes:** a colleague claiming a listing owned by their own company domain is approved directly, with no 72-hour notice.
- **FREEMAIL additions:** hushmail, passinbox, atomicmail, fastmail, tutanota, pm.me, duck.com, rocketmail, aim, yahoo/hotmail/live .co.uk, mail.ru, qq.com and 163.com.
- **Already supported:** several listings per account. Every listing with the same owner email shows on one account.
