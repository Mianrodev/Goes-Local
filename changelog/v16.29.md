# v16.29–v16.30-shared (6 Oct 2026)

## "List your business" repeat submissions (UPA Entertainment, Miami)
- **Cause:** every submission took 15–18 s, because the team alert went to 7 people one at a time before the "Thanks" page showed. The owner gave up and resubmitted. Four later attempts were cancelled at ~10 s by the browser *after* the alert emails went out but *before* the entry was saved. The result was 6+ alert emails, but only 3 pending entries.
- **Fixes:**
  - Team alerts go out in the background (`ctx.waitUntil`) and in parallel (`notifyAdmins` uses Promise.all), so the "Thanks" page shows straight away. The owner's "listing received" email is also sent in the background.
  - A repeat from the same contact/email within 24 h updates the existing pending entry instead of adding another, sends no second alert or confirmation, and shows "Got it — your details are updated… no need to send it again".
  - The submit button switches to "Sending…" and is disabled after the first click.

## Review cards
- `REVCARD` is one card design for Google reviews, and for pending reviews on the owner's Manage page (with Approve/Reject in the card footer). It has a coloured initial, name, date and stars; text longer than 260 characters is clamped to 5 lines with "Read more / Show less". Two columns on desktop, one on phones; no more card-inside-card double borders.
- The Google rating shows one decimal (5 → 5.0). The "Live from Google" label no longer wraps on phones.
