# v15.95-shared (28 Sep 2026)

Two improvements Eric approved: "Best of" pages (to bring in visitors) and a phone listing page that turns visitors into calls. The number v15.94 was only used on a test copy and never deployed.

## Best-of pages
- **New pages:**
  - `/best`: an index of every list, grouped by main category.
  - `/best/{type}`: the top 10 of a business type across the whole city.
  - `/best/{type}/{neighbourhood}`: the top 10 of that type in one neighbourhood.
- **When a page exists:** only when at least 5 businesses of that type (in that neighbourhood) have a Google rating of 4 stars or more from at least 5 reviews. That gives about 630 pages per city.
  - Unknown or too-thin pages redirect: `/best/{type}` goes to `/best`, and `/best/{type}/{hood}` goes to `/best/{type}`.
- **Ranking:** a weighted rating, `(rating × reviews + 4.3 × 20) / (reviews + 20)`, so a 4.8 with 300 reviews beats a 5.0 with 3. Nobody can pay to be on a list.
- **Each page has:**
  - an H1 like "The 10 best nail salons in Brickell, Miami", with "updated {Month Year}";
  - ranked rows with a Call button;
  - links to the same list in other neighbourhoods and to other lists in the same neighbourhood;
  - a 3-question FAQ;
  - ItemList, BreadcrumbList and FAQPage structured data.
- **The page list:** `computeBestIndex` builds it and saves it to `meta.best_index`. The cron rebuilds it hourly, and it is built on first use if missing. Business types that differ only in capitalisation are merged ("Auto repair shop" and "Auto Repair Shop"). Plurals are handled by `PLURAL`/`BEST_NOUN`: "handyman" becomes "handymen", and words ending in "-ing" become "painting companies".
- **Links to the new pages:**
  - "Best of" added to the main menu, and "Best of {City}" to the footer;
  - `/sitemap-best.xml`, added to the sitemap index;
  - a line in `llms.txt`;
  - category pages: "Top-rated {category}" buttons;
  - sub-category pages: a "See the top 10 …" box, specific to the neighbourhood when one is filtered;
  - neighbourhood pages: "Top-rated in {hood}";
  - listing pages: a "See the top 10 {type} in {hood}" box.

## Phone listing page
- **Call and Directions bar** pinned to the bottom of the screen on phones (720px wide or less).
- **Rating and phone number under the business name** for every listing. Unclaimed listings used to show no rating at all; the rating now says "Google rating" when the listing isn't claimed.
- **No empty photo banner:** when there's no cover photo, the orange placeholder banner is hidden on phones and reduced to a thin strip on desktop.
- **"Add to Home Screen" pop-up** shows only from a visitor's second visit (counted in `localStorage`), not on the first page they open.
- **Messages to unclaimed businesses:**
  - The "Send an enquiry" form now shows on every listing. The old button on unclaimed listings only showed a "can't send" pop-up.
  - Messages for businesses without an owner email are saved to the CRM as before. They are now also emailed to a new "Customer messages" notification list (`notify_enquiry`, template `admin_enquiry`), so the team can pass them on and invite the business to claim.
  - The list defaults to `ADMIN_NOTIFY_EMAIL`. Orlando doesn't have that setting, so set recipients in Admin → Email templates & test.
