# v15.93-shared (25 Sep 2026)

Scraped contact details that aren't the business's.

- **Junk emails are hidden on listing pages, and no claim invites are sent to them.** An email counts as junk if it matches a known pattern or is shared by unrelated listings. Known patterns:
  - error trackers such as Sentry and wixpress;
  - template placeholders such as `hi@mystore.com` and `filler@godaddy.com`;
  - `first.last@company.com`;
  - image file names.

  An email is shared by unrelated listings when four or more listings carry it, they have at least three different names, and the email's domain isn't their website's domain. This catches the font designers' credits pulled from websites. A booking or link-in-bio platform's own address (Booksy, Vagaro, Linktree…) also counts as junk.

  The shared list is rebuilt after every sync pass (`computeBadEmails` saves it to `meta.bad_emails`). It covers roughly 940 Miami and 850 Orlando listings.
  - `claimTier` no longer treats a junk email as "matches the email on the listing".
  - A leading `u003e` (an escaped ">") is removed from emails.
- **Junk websites are hidden:** the literal "No Website" (about 2,800 Miami listings), and bare platform home pages such as `https://www.instagram.com`, `https://linktr.ee` and `https://sites.google.com` with no page path. Real profile links, like `instagram.com/joespizza`, are kept.
- The owner-edit form shows these fields blank, so an owner who saves types in their real details.
- `/admin/logos` also reports the junk-email count.
- "About this business": the article before the category is now "a" or "an" as needed ("an Automotive Repair & Services business").
