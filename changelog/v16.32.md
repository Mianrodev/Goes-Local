## Statewide trade sites (area word, areas by town, zip starts)
Lets the shared code run a statewide trade directory (first one: a Florida plumbers site, on its own Worker, D1 and GHL account like any city). Nothing changes for Miami, Orlando or Tampa: with none of the new settings set, every page reads and links exactly as before.

### New optional settings (Cloudflare Variables)
| Variable | Example | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| `CITY_AREA_WORD` | `City` | What the site calls its areas. Menus, headings, footer, breadcrumbs, filters, admin pages and llms.txt use it. Pages move to `/city/{slug}` and `/cities`; old `/neighbourhood…` links 301 there. Plural is automatic (`City` → `Cities`), or set `CITY_AREA_WORDS`. |
| `CITY_AREAS_FROM` | `city` | Each listing goes under the town in its own address (GHL `city`), not a zip mapping. New towns are added automatically during sync (only for zips allowed by `CITY_VALID_ZIPS`). Zip mappings made in Admin still win. |
| `CITY_VALID_ZIPS` | `32,33,34` | Now also accepts zip starts (`32` or `32*`) as well as full zips, so a whole state fits in one line. |
| `CITY_HEADLINE` | `Find Trusted Plumbers in Florida` | Home page heading. |
| `CITY_NOUN` | `Plumbers` | Area page heading and Google title: "Plumbers in Tampa". |
| `CITY_SEARCH_HINT` | `Leak, drain, water heater…` | Grey hint text in the search box. |

### Other fixes
- A business whose area has the same name as its town no longer reads "in Tampa, Tampa".
- Statewide sites don't show "Florida, FL" in the search box or "Tampa, Florida, FL" in area descriptions.
- Blog side column recognises links to both `/neighbourhood/…` and the site's own area pages.

### Categories
Categories still come from each listing's business type. `seed/plumbing-categories.sql` maps the common plumbing types to plumbing categories (Plumbers, Drain Cleaning, Water Heaters, …). Staff can change these in Admin → Content → Categories.

Unit checks: Miami defaults unchanged (zip mapping, name matching, nav), statewide matching by town, zip starts, town name capitals.

## Free-listings-only switch, home-services categories, NY/IL/GA sites
Three new statewide home-services sites on the shared code, free listings only: homeservicesinny.com, homeservicesinil.com, homeservicesinga.com. Each has its own Worker (`homeservices-{ny,il,ga}-api`), D1 (`homeservices-{ny,il,ga}-db`, eastern North America) and KV. Nothing changes for Miami, Orlando or Tampa unless they set the new Variables.

### New optional settings
| Variable | Effect |
|---|---|
| `CITY_FREE_ONLY=1` | Free listings only. Pricing leaves the menu, footer and sitemap. `/pricing`, `/advertise`, `/featured`, `/manage/upgrade` and `/manage/advertise` redirect home (or to `/manage`). Home banner slides that link to paid pages are skipped, empty category banners show nothing instead of "Advertise here", the owner dashboard drops its upgrade banner and "Plus benefit" notes, and the About page says listing is free. Any other link to those pages is hidden. |
| `CITY_LOGO_URL` | Logo picture for the header. `none` = text logo (brand name + tagline, house icon). An image uploaded in Admin → Images still wins. Unset keeps today's default. |

### Categories
`seed/home-services-categories.sql` (replaces the plumbing-only seed) maps about 100 Google business types into 18 home-services categories: Plumbing, Drain & Sewer, Water Heaters, Heating & Air Conditioning, Electricians, Roofing & Gutters, Handyman & Remodeling, Cleaning, Pest Control, Lawn/Landscaping & Trees, and more. Plumbing is first, and new trades fall into place as their listings arrive in GHL. Staff can change the mapping in Admin → Content → Categories.

### Site settings (dashboard Variables)
`CITY_NAME` New York / Illinois / Georgia, `CITY_STATE` NY / IL / GA, `CITY_BRAND` "Home Services in NY/IL/GA", `CITY_AREA_WORD=City`, `CITY_AREAS_FROM=city`, `CITY_VALID_ZIPS` (NY `10,11,12,13,14`; IL `60,61,62`; GA `30,31,398,399`), `CITY_FREE_ONLY=1`, `CITY_LOGO_URL=none`, headline, noun and search hint. GHL settings (`GHL_LOCATION_ID`, `GHL_API_TOKEN`, sending email) are added once the GHL accounts exist.

## State wording and pictures
State wording for the statewide sites (Eric: keep the Goes Local structure, make the content state-specific). Everything here switches on only with `CITY_AREAS_FROM=city`; Miami, Orlando and Tampa read exactly as before.

### Wording
- Home: "N listings in M cities", "Our busiest services across {state}", "Open right now across {state}", "M cities across {state}. Pick yours to see who is close by.", owner box "so customers across {state} can reach you first", trust card "Statewide coverage".
- Categories menu promo: "Find trusted {noun} anywhere in {state}." (was the Miami "leaky tap to a great espresso" line).
- Footer: "An independent directory of {noun} across {state}…" (was "helping neighbours…").
- Category pages: "Plumbing in New York" (was "New York, NY"). Best-of descriptions drop the doubled state too.
- Listing pages: "Buffalo, NY" (was "Buffalo, Buffalo" and "Buffalo, New York"). "More … in Buffalo" lists the same type in the same town first, then statewide ("More … in New York") instead of "nearby" picks from across the state.
- About page: the license check points to `CITY_LICENSE` when set (was always Florida's DBPR).
- Add-your-business form: the category list is the site's own categories (from Admin → Categories), and the "Featured slots" tile is hidden on free-only sites.

### Pictures
- Statewide sites no longer get Miami's starter photos (hero slides, menu promo, owner box, About, Best-of header). They show the generated colour artwork until staff upload their own state photos in Admin → Homepage hero / Images.
- City artwork icons on statewide sites skip palm trees, sun and sailboats.

### New optional setting
`CITY_LICENSE`: where to check a contractor's license, e.g. "your city or county licensing office (in New York City, the Department of Buildings)".
