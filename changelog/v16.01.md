# v16.01-shared (28 Sep 2026)

Best-of changes requested by Eric.

- **City-wide lists only.** There are no more neighbourhood lists and no "Area" chips. `/best/{type}/{neighbourhood}` now 301-redirects to `/best/{type}`. The sitemap lists only city-wide pages. The "Top-rated in {neighbourhood}" box on neighbourhood pages is gone, and the "See the top 10" box on listing and business-type pages always links to the city-wide list.
- **Only business types with enough businesses.** A list exists only when at least 20 businesses of that type have a 4★+ rating from 5+ reviews (`BEST_LIST_MIN`). That is about 100 lists per city, down from about 200; small ones like "car dealers (7)" are gone. Removed lists 301-redirect to `/best`. `bestIndex` also applies this rule to the saved list straight away, without waiting for the hourly rebuild.
- **Claimed businesses stand out** in the top 10. They get a teal border and background tint, a "Verified by the owner" badge, and their own photo when they have one. Their rank does not change.
- **Unclaimed rows** get a small "Is this your business? Claim it free →" link to that listing's claim page, to encourage claims.
- The Best-of home page header now reads "Only businesses rated 4★+" instead of the neighbourhood count, and "N areas" is removed from the cards.
