# v16.42-shared — one shared code for every site

From this release, Orlando, Miami, Tampa and the six Home Services state sites (NY, IL, GA, NC, MA, PA) all run this same worker.js. Statewide behaviour switches on only through Variables (CITY_AREA_WORD, CITY_AREAS_FROM, CITY_FREE_ONLY, CITY_NOUN), which the city sites don't set.

Main's v16.39 is included (numbered v16.39 on main; the state sites had already used that number).

## Main's notes

## v16.39-shared — services sign-off on business emails, different visitor counts per city

Includes v16.38 (Google Analytics hosts allowed in the CSP).

- **Services sign-off on every email the website sends to businesses.** Requested by Eric's boss on 9 Oct; the wording is word for word from his email.
  - The block starts "Let's help more customers find—and choose—you." It lists the seven services and ends with "Reply … 'STRATEGY'".
  - It sits under a divider, after the email's own message and button, and is also added to the plain-text version.
  - Added in one place (`sendTplEmail` → `withPitch`), so every template gets it: sign-in links and codes, listing live, claim emails, listing received and new enquiry.
  - Not added to team-only notices (`[Admin] …`) or to the three emails that go to customers instead of business owners (`review_reply`, `news_update`, `review_request`, listed in `PITCH_SKIP`).
  - Replies go to the contact's GHL conversation, because emails are sent through GHL Conversations.
- **"Today's visitors" was the same number on every city.**
  - The counter now seeds from the date plus the site's own address, so each city shows its own number.
  - Optional Variable `CITY_VISITS` (default 4600) sets roughly where a city's counter ends the day, for example a lower figure for a smaller city.
- **Claimed listings with no owner email get the email on file (Eric, 9 Oct).**
  - At the end of every sync pass, up to 25 listings tagged `claimed` that have no owner email but do have an email on their contact get that email as the owner email.
  - It is written to GHL (owner email custom field, its own call) and to D1, so the owner can sign in and manage the listing.
  - On 9 Oct this applied to only 1 of 18 such listings (Eco Beauty MedSpa Miami). The other 17 Miami ones and Orlando's URPhone Store have no email anywhere, neither on the contact nor in any claim. They fill automatically once Sales adds an email in GHL.
