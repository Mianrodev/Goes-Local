# v16.21-shared (5 Oct 2026)

"List Your Business" (/add) is now an ad landing page, for every city. Built from the mockup Eric approved (form in the first screen). v16.19 and v16.20 were used only on test copies.

- **First screen:**
  - City photo (the busiest neighbourhood's own picture) with a live "N people visited today" badge.
  - Headline "{City} customers are looking. Make sure they find you."
  - 3 benefits, and photos of real claimed businesses with "Join N {City} businesses".
  - The form card sits beside the headline on desktop and right under it on phones.
- **2-step form, same fields and same POST to /api/lead:**
  - Step 1: business name, category, phone, ZIP, email.
  - Step 2: address, neighbourhood, years, website, services, description, owner name/email/phone, referral.
  - Owner email/phone are pre-filled from the business ones (editable).
  - Step 2's required fields only count once it's shown.
  - Without JavaScript both steps show as one form.
  - UTM/attribution hidden fields unchanged.
- **Rest of the page:**
  - Stats bar: visitors today, listings, neighbourhoods, "1–2 days".
  - Two "Example" product pictures: #1 in search results; an enquiry arriving.
  - 3 steps, 4 FAQs and a final call-to-action.
  - Sticky "Add my business — free" button on phones, hidden while the form is on screen.
- Default page title/description (SEOTXT "add") now reads "List your {City} business free…"; Admin → SEO overrides still win.
- Tested: Continue with empty fields stays on step 1; submit posts every field exactly as before (verified with the POST intercepted, no test lead created).
