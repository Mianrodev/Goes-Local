# v16.75-shared — a sales "Verified" only counts once the owner signs in

Eric (23 Oct): sales reps were filling targets by setting Owner Email + "Listing Verified" without reaching the
business. Keep the same process and the same email, but the listing must not be claimed (nor get the claimed tags)
until the owner signs in for the first time with the code we email them. Answers: paid tags wait too; one reminder
after 3 days; leave listings already claimed as they are.

## Change (cold-call path of applyListingVerification only)
- Held when there is no claim/add request from the owner on the site and the listing isn't already claimed:
  - GHL tags: `awaiting-owner-login` added; no `claimed` / `Recently Claimed` / `featured` / `premium` yet
    (any stray claimed tags removed). Listing written unclaimed.
  - New D1 table `owner_pending(ghl_id, email, tier, created_at, reminded_at, activated_at)`; email = Owner Email,
    else the business email (or filled in later when the "email added afterwards" job sends the invite).
  - The same claim_invite email goes out as before; GHL note explains it counts only after the owner's first sign-in.
- `activateOwner()` runs on every sign-in (`startSession`): pending rows for that email get claimed + Recently Claimed
  (+ featured / premium for Plus / Pro), `awaiting-owner-login` removed, Owner Email set, listing claimed, GHL note
  "The owner signed in for the first time on …".
- Staff addresses as Owner Email (`OURMAIL`: the site's domain, mianrosystems.com) never count: no invite, tag
  `owner-email-internal`, GHL note, dev-team alert. (QA contacts tagged qa-test are exempt for testing.)
- Reminder: hourly job sends `claim_invite_reminder` once, 3 days after, to owners who haven't signed in.
- Sync catch-up (lvFix) skips held contacts. Claims and new listings requested by owners on the site, and listings
  already claimed, behave as before.

Also includes v16.74 from the states session (profile greeting uses the listing's Owner Name).
