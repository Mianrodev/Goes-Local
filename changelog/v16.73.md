# v16.73-shared — "Finish your listing" counts photos added in the photo slots

Found in the North Carolina demo QA (23 Oct): an owner who uploaded a profile photo still saw "4 of 5 done · Add photos or your logo". `LISTCHECK` looked for `b.sprofile`/`b.scover`, but listing rows keep those photos in `b.slots` (`profile`, `cover`, `store`, `location`, `extra`). It now counts any of them. Shared fix, for cities and states.

## Status

Deployed to the six state sites. The cities get it on their next deploy.
