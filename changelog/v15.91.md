# v15.91-shared (25 Sep 2026)

Duplicate listings, option (a), approved by Eric.

- **One listing per business.** Listings with the same name + phone + address (case/punctuation ignored) are grouped. The site keeps the best one: claimed, then Plus, then paid, then the most reviews, then the highest rating, then the oldest. The other copies are hidden. They are removed from the `businesses` table, so lists, search, sitemaps and counts drop them, and they are recorded in the new `dup_hidden` table. Their old web addresses 301 to the kept listing through `slug_redirects`. Claimed or paid copies are never hidden. Nothing changes in GoHighLevel.
- The listing sync skips hidden copies. A copy comes back automatically if it becomes claimed or paid, or if its kept listing disappears; it may be re-hidden later if it is still a duplicate. `insertOne` (explicit add/claim) also brings a copy back.
- Runs at the end of every full sync pass (`dedupeListings`), and on demand at **/admin/duplicates** (admin only). **/admin/duplicates?csv=1** downloads the list of hidden copies with their CRM ids and old/new addresses, for CRM cleanup.
- Before the change: Miami had 1,909 groups with ~2,900 extra copies and Orlando 725 groups with ~830 extra copies. None had reviews, photos or followers.
