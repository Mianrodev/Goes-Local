## v16.35-shared — "#1 in {city}" bragging everywhere, no enquiry form on unclaimed listings

Includes v16.34 (the "Open now" section is hidden when nothing is open).

### Bragging

Requested by Eric and his boss on 8 Oct; they chose headline option D. All wording uses the city's own name from Variables via `BRAG()`, which returns "{city}'s #1 Local Business Directory".

- **Top bar on every page:** "🏆 {city}'s #1 Local Business Directory · 100% local · Free to search, free to list".
  - Replaces the made-up "Today's visitors" counter.
  - `VISITORBAR` is removed; the bar is now `BRAGBAR()`.
- **Home hero:**
  - "#1 in {city}" badge.
  - Headline: "Born in {city}. Built for {city}. #1 in {city}."
  - A one-line promise under the headline.
  - Three proof ticks (100% {city} businesses · Real Google reviews · Owner-verified listings) replace the listings/categories counter.
- **New band under the hero:** "Why {city} searches here first" (`WHYBAND()`), with four points: 100% {city} · Real reviews · Straight to the business · Free, always.
- **Home section headings:**
  - What {city} is searching for
  - Not sure what it's called? Start here.
  - Open now, near you
  - Every corner of {city}, covered
  - Get found on {city}'s #1 local directory. Free.
- **Category tiles:** no longer show listing counts.
- **Footer line and Organization schema:** "{city}'s #1 Local Business Directory, helping neighbours…".
- **Home Google title and description:** the defaults now use the claim. The SEO team's overrides in Admin → SEO still win: Orlando (title and description) and Miami (description).
- **Other pages:**
  - /add: the badge and stats strip say "#1 local directory in {city}". The made-up visitor count is gone.
  - /claim and /pricing: the line above the headline mentions the claim.
  - Results pages: "🏆 {city}'s #1 Local Business Directory" shows next to the count.

### Unclaimed listings: no enquiry form

Customers, and owners who should have claimed their listing, were sending enquiries that only reached our team.

- **Listing page side box for businesses with no owner (`LN2CONTACT`):**
  - A big Call button.
  - "Is this your business? Wrong address, phone or details? Claim it free…" with a Claim button.
  - Claimed listings keep the enquiry form.
- **About text for unclaimed listings:** "Call … to reach them directly. Own this business? Claim it free to update these details."
- **Result cards:** unclaimed businesses show "View details" instead of "Send enquiry".
- **The /api/lead handler is unchanged,** so an enquiry sent from an old cached page is still saved.
