# v16.00-shared (28 Sep 2026)

Header and Best-of page redesign, requested by Eric. The numbers v15.98 and v15.99 were only used on a test copy and never deployed.

## Header
- The "Best of" menu item is now "{City}'s Best" ("Miami's Best", "Orlando's Best") with a trophy icon, highlighted in the brand orange. `BEST_LABEL()` builds the label from the city name.
- "For Businesses" is removed from the menu; the "List Your Business" button covers it.
- **Phone and tablet header (1000px wide or less):**
  - The menu button, logo and "List Your Business" button now sit on one row with 16px side padding. Before, the logo dropped to a second row and the buttons to a third.
  - On narrow phones "Log in" is hidden from the top bar and appears as the first item in the menu instead ("Log in / My account", `.mnav-only`).
- The footer link reads "{City}'s Best →".

## Best-of pages
- **List pages:**
  - A picture header (category photo with a dark overlay) holding the title and three small facts: how it is ranked, how many businesses qualify, and when it was updated.
  - A scrollable row of area chips to switch neighbourhood.
  - Ranked cards with gold, silver and bronze badges for the top 3, a "Top pick" label on #1, a star row with the rating and review count, the business type, neighbourhood and a Verified tag, and Call / View buttons.
  - On desktop, a sidebar holds "How we rank", "More top-rated lists" and a "claim your listing" box. The long paragraph and the list of lists at the bottom are gone.
  - The FAQ stays at the bottom, collapsed.
- **Best-of home page (`/best`):**
  - A header with a search box that filters the lists as you type.
  - "Most popular lists" as 10 picture cards.
  - Every list grouped by category in tidy boxes: the first 8 lists show, and a "Show all N" button reveals the rest.
- Stays readable on phones: cards stack, buttons go full width, and the picture cards show in two columns.
