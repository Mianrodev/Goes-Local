# v16.26-shared (6 Oct 2026)

Fix: email-campaign leads were being tagged "seoinbound".

- **Cause:** a recipient who opens our campaign email in Gmail on the web and clicks through arrives with referrer `mail.google.com`. `leadChannelTag` saw "google" and called it a search. Yahoo Mail and Outlook behaved the same.
- **Webmail referrers no longer count as search:** `WEBMAIL_SRC` covers mail.google.com, mail.yahoo, outlook/live/hotmail, ProtonMail, iCloud, Zoho, AOL, and GHL/Mailgun link trackers.
- **`addSeoInbound(env, cid)`:** never adds "seoinbound" to a contact that already has "emailreplied". All three tagging spots use it: claim, add-a-business, and pending-listing publish.
- **Ongoing cleanup:** every sync pass removes "seoinbound" from any contact that also has "emailreplied" (up to 25 per page). Email leads are tagged by the GHL workflow only.
- **`/admin/seotags?audit=1`** (read-only) lists every seoinbound contact in four groups: has emailreplied, clicked from an email app, search/AI source on record, no proof either way. `&fix=1` removes the tag from the first two groups only.

## v16.27-shared
- Gmail/Outlook/Yahoo phone apps (referrer `com.google.android.gm` and similar) count as email clicks too. The audit no longer mistakes the Gmail app's "google" for a Google search.
