# v16.13-shared (29 Sep 2026)

New results pages, used by category, business-type, neighbourhood and search pages. Built from the mockup Eric approved (A), plus the "Advertise here" banner (C). The number v16.12 was only used on a test copy.

## Top of the page
- **Title row:** the page title and result count on the left, and the search box on the right, on one line.
- **Banner:** kept at its 8:3 size so it can still be sold.
  - Paid slides get a "Featured" label and an "Advertise here →" link.
- **Unsold banner (C):** it no longer fills itself with unpaid businesses. The autofill is switched off, so it shows only the paid slides plus the "Ad space available — Your business, front and center in {category}" slide, now with a coral "Advertise here →" button.
  - Categories with banners set by staff in `/admin/banners` are unchanged.

## Filters
- **Business types:** one sideways-scrolling row of buttons with counts. It was two rows plus a sidebar list.
- **Filter bar:** small pills for Neighbourhood, Rating, Status and Sort, each opening a short list. On phones the list opens as a bottom sheet.
  - A "🏆 Top 10 lists" pill replaces the "Top-rated … in Miami" box.
- **Sidebar filter list removed.**

## Result rows (`RESROW`)
- **Each row shows:** a small photo, badges (Verified/Unclaimed, Top Rated, PLUS/PRO, paid tier label), the name, the rating with review count and type, the neighbourhood and address, and a "Claim it free" link on unclaimed rows.
- **One Call button and one View button.** The phone number, rating and Directions used to appear 2–3 times per card.
- **#1–#3 numbers** on the first page when sorted by rating or reviews.
- **Paid ad-slot rows** keep their coloured ring and label.

## Right column
- **"Popular areas":** the top 6 neighbourhoods for this page, with counts.
- **Claim box:** "Own a business in {category}? Claim free".
- **Map:** `RESMAP` draws OpenStreetMap tiles with numbered pins. It stays hidden for now, because no listing has latitude/longitude synced yet.

## Search pages
- The sidebar used to show whole-city category counts (e.g. "Beauty & Personal Care 3,170" on a plumber search). It is now a "Browse a category" pill without counts.
