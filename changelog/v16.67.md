# v16.67-shared — business emails come from the assigned sales rep, not "No Reply"

Eric (22 Oct): "the emails are going out from No Reply, can we ensure that these emails go out with the user who is
assigned to that business?"

## Change
- Every template email to a business (claim invite, verified, listing live, reminders, …) now looks up the GHL
  contact's **Assigned User** and sends as that rep:
  - rep's address on the site's own domain (zack.m@miamigoeslocal.com, max.r@orlandogoeslocal.com) →
    `From: Zack Mawia <zack.m@miamigoeslocal.com>`; replies go to the rep's mailbox.
  - rep's address on another domain (…@mianrosystems.com) → `From: Rob Smith | Miami Goes Local <site address>`.
    Mail is sent through the site's GHL sending domain (lc.<site>); miamigoeslocal.com has DMARC p=reject, so a
    From on a different domain would be rejected or land in spam.
  - The message is also attributed to that rep in GHL Conversations (`userId`).
- Unchanged: no rep assigned, rep lookup fails, or GHL refuses the rep sender → the site's usual sender
  (the send is retried once from the site address). Login codes and team-only notices keep the site sender.
- Rep details cached 6 h per worker.
- Also includes v16.66 (not yet live): email template saving fix, {{city}}/{{website}} placeholders, claim-invite line.

## Data
- Miami: ~17,000 business contacts have an assigned user (mostly @miamigoeslocal.com reps). Orlando reps are
  @orlandogoeslocal.com.
