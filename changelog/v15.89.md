# v15.89-shared (25 Sep 2026)

Cost plan Phase E step 12 — cron cadence.

- The Google-reviews refresh, Listing-URL write-back and Recently-Claimed expiry batches now run at most **once an hour** (gate: `meta.cron_hourly_at`) instead of every 5-minute tick, with larger batches (60 / 100 / 100) so throughput is about the same. The Listing-URL batch does two whole-table scans per run (`(cs||'/'||slug)<>url_synced_path` can't use an index), which was ~45M rows/day on Orlando alone.
- The listing sync itself is unchanged (it already skips when data is < 10 min old); the claim-invite job is limited per business since v15.85.
