# v16.06-shared (29 Sep 2026)

`/admin/seotags` can now find older SEO leads from their GHL tags. Before the site recorded visit sources, the only record of where a lead came from was its GHL tags.

## Modes
- **`?list=1`:** read-only. Walks every GHL contact and counts the tags and contact "source" values that look like lead-source markers. `&re=` sets a different pattern.
- **`?preview=1`:** read-only. Sorts every website lead into three groups:
  - **Sure:** a tag says search or AI, such as `source-chatgptcom`, `source-…google…` or `heard-about-us: google search`.
  - **Likely:** the lead came in through the website (`pending-listing`, `claim-request`, or source "add-a-business") with no sign of email, a sales call or social media.
  - **Not SEO:** `emailreplied`, a "Call with…" or demo source, or `heard-about-us: social media`.
- **`?apply=sure` or `?apply=sure,likely`:** adds `seoinbound` to those groups. Tags are only ever added.
- **No parameters:** the v16.05 backfill from the site's own records.

## Notes
- The site's GHL token has no scope for `/locations/{id}/tags`, so the tool walks the contacts instead. That's about 170 pages for Miami.
- **Miami preview on 29 Sep:** 12 sure (8 already tagged), 17 likely and 4 not SEO.
