# v16.03-shared (29 Sep 2026)

Real default photos are in, and the artwork icon no longer covers card titles.

## Code
- **Business-type artwork:** the icon now sits top-right, so it no longer covers the title on the Best-of cards. All other artwork keeps the icon in the middle.
- **Art links** now carry `?v=2`, so browsers drop their 7-day copy of the old artwork.

## Content (added through Admin, not code)
- **Neighbourhood photos (Wikimedia Commons):** 25 in Orlando and 7 in Miami, stored as `hood-{slug}` with credits.
  - Hollywood (Miami) uses the Hollywood Beach boardwalk photo.
  - The other 10 keep the designed artwork because no photo of the right place was found: Christmas, College Park, SoDo, Colonialtown, Pine Hills, South Semoran, Alafaya, Williamsburg, Clermont and Silver Bluff.
- **Business-type photos (Openverse: Flickr, rawpixel, StockSnap, WordPress):** 88 types, stored as `type-{slug}` in each city that has the type.
  - The other 56 types keep the designed artwork. The photos found were off-topic, showed brand names, or were old postcards.
- **Credits:** all photos are credited on `/photo-credits`. Any of them can be swapped in Admin → Category images.
- **StockSnap blocks downloads from the Worker (HTTP 403).** Those photos were imported through Openverse's copy of the same image (`/v1/images/{id}/thumb/?full_size=true`).

## Database fix
- The `credit` and `credit_url` columns from v16.02 were missing on both databases, even though `/admin/migrate` had reported OK. They were added by hand with `wrangler d1 execute`.
- Until then, Admin → Category images → upload failed on both sites, because it writes `credit=''`.
