# v15.92-shared (25 Sep 2026)

Photo and neighbourhood cleanup, as approved by Eric.

- **Wrong listing pictures are hidden.** Some pictures scraped automatically from websites were the wrong image: Instagram's icon, a Linktree, Vagaro, Booksy, Fresha or Bluepillow logo, a parked-domain logo, and so on. An image is treated as wrong when either of these is true:
  - five or more differently-named businesses share it, and it isn't hosted on their own website domain; or
  - most of the listings that share it have a booking or link-in-bio platform as their website.
  The list of wrong images is rebuilt after every sync pass (`computeBadLogos` saves it to `meta.bad_logos`). Real chain logos are kept (Mavis, CVS, AdventHealth…). The affected listings show their category photo instead. This covers about 275 Miami and 200 Orlando listings.
- **Dead pictures are hidden.** A new hourly job (`logoCheckBatch`, table `logo_check`) checks about 120 listing images an hour. It marks an image dead if it returns a 4xx, points to a host that doesn't exist, or returns a web page instead of an image. Each image is re-checked every 30 days. `ROWOF` hides both kinds of picture. The data isn't changed, so the next scan brings a picture back once it's fixed.
- **/admin/logos** (admin only) reruns the scan and shows the counts; add `?check` to also check 40 images right away.
- **The new-neighbourhood queue ignores out-of-area zips.** This applies to cities without `CITY_VALID_ZIPS`, currently Orlando. A zip can only enter the queue if its first three digits cover at least 5% of the city's own listings; for Orlando that is 327, 328 and 347. This is how Miami zips became Orlando neighbourhoods.
- **Data fix on Orlando (not code):** removed the neighbourhoods Aventura, Kendall, Miami Gardens and Westchester, their zip mappings, and any pending queue entries outside the area.
