# v16.48-shared — emails no longer fail on business contacts with no email

## The problem

On 14 Oct, 22 of the 70 verification invites failed (9 Orlando, 13 Miami). GHL answered `400 "Contact has no email"` (CONVERSATIONS_MSG_NO_EMAIL).
- `sendEmail()` was sending through the business's GHL contact, and many business contacts keep their email in a custom field rather than the main email field.
- The same could silently stop any website email sent through a business contact while going to someone else's address: "claim approved", "listing live", the reminder, and the transfer notice.

## The fix

If GHL refuses a send through a given contact, `sendEmail()` finds or creates the recipient's own contact by email address and sends through that instead. The retry is logged as "retrying via the recipient's own contact".

## QA

The 51/51 local suite passes.

## Follow-up after deploy

The 22 failed invites get their `[invited]` mark cleared and are sent again through `/admin/verify-invites`.
